## Usage example

`ChartFrame` is the card every chart draws in with `variant="a2ui"`. Put any content inside; it can use the A2UI colour variables (`var(--a2-chart-1)` to `var(--a2-chart-6)`, `var(--a2-muted-fg)`, ...), which follow light and dark mode. The legend shows at the top right when it has more than one item.

<Demo of={DemoExample} />

## Aside and footer

`aside` replaces the legend, for a summary value or a key. `footer` goes under the content.
