## Usage example

The example below shows the official A2UI sample quote. `sample` adds a badge that marks the numbers as illustrative. The sparkline is a [SparkAreaChart](/components/spark-chart) in the theme success or error colour, with a dashed line at the first price.

<Demo of={DemoExample} />

## A2UI look

Set `variant="a2ui"` to draw the A2UI card.

<Demo of={DemoA2ui} />

## Falling price and other currencies

A negative `change` turns the change and the sparkline to the error colour. `currency` and `locale` set how the price reads.
